Provider Variation & Fixed-Effects Analysis

Research question: How much variation in early palliative-care billing is associated with physicians and healthcare organizations after patient case-mix adjustment?

Simulated data structure

LevelUnitsSelected variables
Patient20,000Age, sex, race/ethnicity, socioeconomic measures, cancer type, comorbidity
Physician900Age group, sex, integration status, prior referral
Organization120Size and palliative-medicine specialist availability

Analytical approach

The program estimates separate physician and organization fixed-effects linear probability models with clustered standard errors, compares residual variation with a case-mix model, and fits logistic regression for adjusted associations and average marginal effects.

Interpretation

Fixed effects describe adjusted within-dataset provider variation; they do not by themselves identify causal provider effects or establish quality rankings. All data are synthetic.
