CGM Randomized Trial Analysis

Research question: Does continuous glucose monitoring improve six-month HbA1c compared with standard care?

Design and methods

A synthetic two-arm randomized trial of 200 adults includes baseline, three-month, and six-month HbA1c. The primary ANCOVA adjusts the six-month outcome for baseline HbA1c, age, and sex.

Primary result

The positive contrast indicates higher six-month HbA1c under standard care in this simulated dataset. It demonstrates the workflow and is not clinical evidence.
